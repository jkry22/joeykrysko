Projects and Experience:

Goodwill Keystone Design Project

Goodwill Keystone sponsored a design challenge for my engineering design class. Students in every section attempted to solve one of Goodwill's main problems. My group decided to improve upon Goodwill's current Z-rack design by making it more spacially efficient. We also proposed an alternative to the current process which made use of the features on our improved rack.

At the Penn State Engineering Expo we presented our design to engineering professors and Goodwill Keystone Representatives, and we placed as finalists in the design challenge.

Within the group, I was primarily in charge of creating models of the new rack design using Solidworks. The images to the right show some of the models, and they also demonstrate how the rack can rotate for more efficient storage inside of Goodwill's warehouses and shipping trucks.

Gilbane 30th Street Station Redevelopment Project

I worked as a Construction Engineering Intern for Gilbane Building Company on the 30th Street Station Redevelopment Project in Philadelphia. During my time with Gilbane, I worked closely with the project managers and field engineers. My role as a construction intern involved facilitating communication between the project management team and subcontractors, reviewing and approving subcontractor submittals, and helping solve RFIs and complex field problems with the field team.

I also gained experience with useful softwares such as Procore, Bluebeam, Microsoft Excel, and OpenSpace. I learned how to read and markup construction drawings in Bluebeam and ensure they matched with field conditions, and I learned how to record and map field progress using OpenSpace.

My internship concluded with a presentation of a mock bid proposal. I collaborated with other interns to create a bid proposal for one of Gilbane's projects. The project scope included creating an estimate, project schedule, logistics plan, and safety plan. My team was also responsible for creating our own mock construction company and marketing our company to Gilbane executives.
